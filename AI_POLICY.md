# AI Usage Policy

AI assistance is welcome, but you are responsible for everything you submit.

* **Understand your changes.** Review the entire diff and be able to explain how it works without
  relying on AI. Passing tests or an AI review does not replace your understanding.

* **Verify the problem and the fix.** Confirm bugs through reproduction, tests, logs, or code analysis.
  State what you actually tested, on which versions, and what remains unchecked. Never invent results.

* **Keep changes focused.** Follow the project's architecture and conventions. Discuss large features
  or cleanups first; do not submit bulk changes just because a tool suggested them.

* **Review everything you publish.** Issues, comments, commit messages, and PR descriptions must be
  accurate, concise, and in your own voice. Do not paste raw AI conversations or unverified reports.
  You are responsible for the origin and licenses of contributed material.

* **Respect maintainer time.** Keep descriptions concise and proportional to the change: no walls of
  text, filler, repetition, or AI slop. Be prepared to answer questions and revise your work. Unsolicited
  automated submissions, repeated unverified contributions, or ignored review feedback may lead to
  closed contributions or an account block. Maintainer-approved automation is permitted.

This project is maintained by people. AI can help you contribute, but it must not shift your work
of understanding and verifying a contribution onto the reviewer.

Inspired by the [GTNH](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GTNH-Dev-Doc/blob/master/AI_POLICY.md)
and [Ghostty](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md) AI policies.
